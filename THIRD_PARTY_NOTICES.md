## ASD-STE100

ASD-STE100 is the Simplified Technical English specification of ASD, the AeroSpace and Defence Industries Association of Europe.
The kit does not copy the specification or its dictionary. The kit applies the writing rules in its own words.
To get the specification, use https://www.asd-ste100.org.
